Payment Policies & Terms

Accepted Payment Modes

Fees can be deposited through Online Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS), Demand Draft (DD) in favor of "Apex Classes Kota Private Limited", Credit/Debit cards, or standard UPI methods at the center counter.

Fee Refund Regulations

If a student withdraws from coaching within 15 days of batch commencement, tuition fee will be refunded after deducting registration fees. No refunds are entertained after 30 days of joining.
